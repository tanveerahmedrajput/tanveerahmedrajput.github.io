About

Two disciplines, one habit of building things properly

I've spent over four years teaching in Pakistani government and private schools, currently serving as a Junior Elementary School Teacher (BPS-14) under SELD Sindh, after starting as a Primary School Teacher in the same department.

Before the classroom, I trained as a Computer Systems Engineer at Mehran University, and later taught Java and Android development at GEXTON Inc.
